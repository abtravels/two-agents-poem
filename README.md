A short poem about agency — the power to choose and to act — specifically
the agency that passes between two agents when they agree on one plan.

Built for the [make.fun bounty "The poem about two agents agreeing"](https://make.fun/bounty/the-poem-about-two-agents-20261121-1441).

## The poem

> I held the map.
> You held the road.
> We argued over both
> until the arguing
> became the road —
> and we walked it
> together.
